normalized low-pole coordinate $u'$ over $k(\tau)$ (Lemma~4.3 of the paper), with
$1\le j\le\lfloor5i/23\rfloor$. Source: \texttt{hprime-canon.txt} (SHA-256 prefix \texttt{14bc240d}),
obtained by the series solve \texttt{m13a\_canon.gp} and by the elimination \texttt{elim\_H.sing};
the two agree in all $49$ coefficients.
